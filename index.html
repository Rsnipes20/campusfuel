THE BUSINESS BEHIND THE PROTOTYPE

A tracker that remembers your routine.

CampusFuel explores a simple idea: make repeat food logging easier for university students. A proposed free tier would cover logging, with optional paid planning features in a real product.

Useful from day one

Searching food, adding servings and seeing totals work without other users. The basic tracker has no direct network effect.

More personal over time

Saved favourites and history reduce repeat effort. Leaving could mean rebuilding routines, a potential switching cost rather than a network effect.

These scenario numbers are invented, not user research or measured savings. They are separate from the working diary.

Convenience is not a guaranteed moat

A rival can copy these features. History can be exported, and people can use multiple trackers. Any lasting advantage would need evidence of trust, useful data and retention.

What a real service would need

Verified nutrition sources, secure accounts, a database, consent and deletion controls, accessible design, backups and ongoing support. This demo sends no data anywhere and offers no medical or dietary advice.
